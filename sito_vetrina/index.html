Ninety is provided by Mariella Farella and built around data minimization and privacy by design. Your personal, health, and sleep data never leaves your devices.

✓ No data collection or storage
✓ No accounts or third-party SDKs
✓ 100% on-device processing

1. Data Collection, Storage, and Accounts

This Privacy Policy describes how the “Ninety” application, provided by Mariella Farella, handles your information. Our approach is strictly based on data minimization and privacy by design: we do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data.

Ninety operates exclusively on-device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal, health, or sleep-related data on our servers or any third-party servers.

  • Ninety does not require user registration or account creation.
  • Ninety does not process payments or use third-party subscription frameworks.

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), since no personal data is collected, stored, or processed on external servers, no personal data processing operations are carried out by the provider outside of your local device.

The entire process of estimating sleep stages and triggering the smart alarm happens locally on your Apple Watch and iPhone.

2. Health Data and Sensors (Apple HealthKit and Core Motion)

To provide its core functionality—the sleep-stage smart alarm—Ninety requires access to specific sensors on your Apple Watch:

  • Heart Rate: Accessed via Apple HealthKit APIs.
  • Motion Data (Accelerometer): Accessed via Core Motion.

This data is read in real time exclusively during a 30-minute window prior to your set “hard deadline.” It is processed by a Machine Learning model embedded directly within the app. Once the alarm trigger is calculated, the real-time data is immediately discarded. Ninety has no data retention period because no data is ever saved.

3. Strict Prohibition on Advertising and Data Brokers

In strict compliance with Apple App Store Guidelines, specifically regarding HealthKit and biometric data:

  • We do not use data obtained from HealthKit or Core Motion for advertising, marketing, or cross-context behavioral tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your health or motion data with data brokers, advertising networks, analytics platforms, or any other third parties.
  • Your biometric data is used solely to provide the smart alarm functionality.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Ninety is completely self-contained. It does not integrate any third-party SDKs (Software Development Kits) for crash reporting, telemetry, advertising tracking, or user profiling.

5. Consent and Revocation

Access to heart rate data requires your explicit consent via the Apple Health app upon first launch. You retain full control over your data. You can revoke this access at any time through the Health app settings on your iPhone. Please note that revoking access will disable Ninety’s ability to detect sleep stages in real time.
